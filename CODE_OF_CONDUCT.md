# Code of conduct

Lotus follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual or violent content.
- No sharing other people's private information, including Warframe account details.

It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer ([@t-2ddy](https://github.com/t-2ddy)) privately through the
repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, and start the title with "Conduct:".
Reports are kept confidential. Anyone who breaks these rules may be warned, or blocked from the
project.
